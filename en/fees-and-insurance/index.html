Good to know

Conditions

  • Missed appointments
    An appointment that is forgotten or not cancelled at least 24 hours in advance will be charged at the rate of the planned consultation.
  • Renewal of therapeutic advice
    No renewal without prior follow-up.
  • Online sessions
    For sessions held online in exceptional cases, payment is made in advance.
Payment & reimbursement

How it works

  • Payment at the practice
    Immediately at the end of the consultation. Cash preferred (please bring the exact amount).
  • TWINT
    Also accepted.
  • Receipt for reimbursement
    The invoice with the receipt for your supplementary insurer is emailed to you as soon as payment is received.
