Cognitive behavioural therapy for insomnia

Have you been sleeping badly, or not at all, for weeks or even years? CBT for insomnia is a structured approach that helps you understand your sleep, change the habits and thoughts that keep insomnia going, and get lasting, restorative sleep back.

Book an appointment
You may recognise yourself if…

When night-time becomes a source of anxiety

  • You take a long time to fall asleep, your mind racing.
  • You wake up at night or too early in the morning and can’t get back to sleep.
  • You feel exhausted during the day: concentration, mood and energy suffer.
  • You dread going to bed.
  • You would like to reduce or avoid sleeping pills (in agreement with your doctor).
“As soon as I lie down, my brain starts spinning.”
“I wake up at 3am, every night.”

The method

How does CBT for insomnia work?

Cognitive behavioural therapy for insomnia (CBT-I) is recognised as a first-line approach for chronic insomnia. It is short, active and structured.

Assess

An assessment of your sleep and a sleep diary kept for a few weeks, to understand your rhythms and what keeps your insomnia going.

Understand & adjust

Key facts about how sleep works, then gradual adjustments to your schedule and habits to consolidate your nights.

Quieten the mind

Working on thoughts and worries about sleep, and learning relaxation techniques for a calmer bedtime.

A holistic approach to sleep

Depending on your situation, CBT for insomnia can be combined with individualised homeopathic follow-up, to also work on the terrain (stress, anxiety, hormonal imbalances, pain…).

  • Individual sessions, in person or by video call
  • Simple exercises to put in place between sessions
  • Progress tracked with the sleep diary
  • Alongside your medical care

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who is CBT for insomnia for?

Adults and teenagers who have suffered from insomnia (difficulty falling asleep, night waking or waking too early) for several weeks or longer, with an impact on their daytime life.

How many sessions are needed?

CBT for insomnia is a short approach: the number of sessions is agreed with you at the first appointment, according to your situation and progress.

Can I keep taking my sleeping pills?

Yes. Any change to a treatment is decided with your doctor. If you wish, and in agreement with them, CBT for insomnia can support a gradual reduction.

What if my sleep problems have a medical cause?

If a medical sleep disorder is suspected (sleep apnoea, for example), I will refer you to your doctor. CBT for insomnia is used alongside a medical assessment.
