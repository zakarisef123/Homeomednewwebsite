Privacy policy

Here we explain what we do with the data we obtain about you via homeomed.ch, in accordance with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where applicable, the GDPR. We clearly state the purposes for which we process personal data, limit its collection to what is necessary for legitimate purposes, take appropriate security measures and respect your right to access your data and have it corrected or deleted.

1. Data processed

We process the data you send us when you contact us by phone, email, WhatsApp or post, as well as the data needed to provide consultations (booking, invoicing, receipts for your insurer). This data is not shared with third parties without your consent, except where required by law.

2. Cookies and analytics

This website uses neither advertising cookies nor analytics tools. Fonts are hosted on our own server: no requests are made to Google Fonts.

3. Third-party services

  • OneDoc: online booking is provided by the OneDoc platform, which processes the data you enter there according to its own privacy policy.

4. Retention period

Data is kept for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, and in accordance with legal retention obligations (in particular accounting and patient-record obligations).

5. Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access or use.

6. Your rights

  • Right of access: you have the right to access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right of rectification: you can complete, correct, delete or block your personal data at any time.
  • Withdrawal of consent: if you have given us your consent, you can withdraw it and have your data deleted.
  • Portability: you can request your personal data and transfer it to another data controller.
  • Objection: you can object to the processing of your data, unless there are legitimate grounds for processing.
