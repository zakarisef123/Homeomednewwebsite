ADHD & homeopathy: understanding each person to help them better

By Paola Orlando16 October 2025

Over the past few weeks I have had many conversations with parents and adults affected by ADHD — attention deficit hyperactivity disorder. And one thing keeps coming up: this diagnosis covers very different realities from one person to another.

Behind ADHD there isn’t a single story, but unique journeys: children, teenagers and adults with their own sensitivities and reactions. And there are also committed families who face a demanding daily life every day.

ADHD: one diagnosis, many experiences

ADHD is a neurodevelopmental condition, usually identified in childhood, but it can also affect teenagers and adults. Its manifestations can vary greatly from one person to another:

  • difficulties with attention and concentration,
  • restlessness or motor hyperactivity,
  • impulsivity, sensory hypersensitivities,
  • sleep problems or tics,
  • significant fatigue, difficulty getting organised, impact at school, at home or socially.

The same diagnosis can therefore cover very varied realities. That is why a single, standardised approach is often not enough.

The classical homeopathy approach: a person-centred view

Every child, teenager and adult living with ADHD has their own way of coping with difficulties: some become agitated, others withdraw; some sleep little, others very badly; some express things intensely, others hardly at all. It is this diversity of profiles and experiences that guides the therapeutic work.

In classical homeopathy, you don’t give “a remedy for ADHD”. You look for the remedy that matches a specific person — their way of reacting, feeling and functioning. This fundamental principle — individualisation — is at the heart of the approach:

Without individualisation, there is no homeopathy.

To achieve this, the first step is an in-depth clinical history and close observation of every detail:

  • how long the symptoms have been present,
  • the context in which they appeared,
  • sleep quality, modalities, emotional reactions,
  • hypersensitivities, triggers,
  • and often also elements linked to pregnancy or early childhood.

Every detail matters. This global understanding makes it possible to develop an individualised therapeutic strategy, adapted to the person and reassessed over time.

A complementary approach, never an exclusive one

Classical homeopathy does not replace existing care. On the contrary, it fits into a global approach, alongside occupational therapy, speech therapy, medical or psychological follow-up. It can help to:

  • better understand the individual terrain,
  • support emotional and functional regulation,
  • strengthen the coherence of the different forms of care,
  • support progress while respecting the follow-up already in place.

In conclusion

ADHD is not a fixed label: it is a lived reality, often complex and demanding, for the people concerned and for those around them. Classical homeopathy offers a detailed, individualised and structured reading, alongside existing care. It never replaces medical or specialist care, but it can become a complementary link in a global, coherent support plan.
