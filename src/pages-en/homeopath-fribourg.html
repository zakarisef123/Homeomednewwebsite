Practical information

Frequently asked questions about the Fribourg practice

Which day do you see patients in Fribourg?

Every Thursday, from 9am to 6pm, by appointment only. On other days I see patients in Pully, or by video call depending on circumstances.

Where is the practice in Fribourg?

At Centre ITS – City Centre branch, Rue de la Banque 4, on the 3rd floor (behind Manor). Access by lift or stairs, no entry code.

How do I get there from Fribourg station?

The practice is a 3-minute walk from the station (main exit). By bus, the Fribourg, St-Pierre stop (lines 1, 2, 3, 5 and 6) is right outside the building.

Where can I park in Fribourg?

The closest car park is Manor. You can also use the Fribourg Centre, Gare, Ancienne Gare or Grand-Places car parks.

Do you consult in English or German?

I consult in English, French and Italian. I’m learning German, but not yet well enough for consultations.
