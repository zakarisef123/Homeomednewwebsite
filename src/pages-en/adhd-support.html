ADHD support

ADHD never concerns only the child: it affects the whole family. I offer support in three parts — for the child, for the parents and for the family — so that everyone finds their place, calm and confidence in daily life.

A demanding daily life, for everyone

Homework that turns into conflict, comments from school, meltdowns, fatigue, guilt… Behind an ADHD diagnosis there is a child with their own way of functioning, and parents doing their best, often on the verge of exhaustion.

My role is to help you understand what is going on, to support the child in an individualised way and to give the whole family some breathing space.

A complementary approach

Always in coordination with existing care

Classical homeopathy does not replace existing care. It fits into a global approach, alongside medical and psychological follow-up, occupational therapy or speech therapy.

  • Individualise: no “ADHD remedy”, but the remedy for this particular person.
  • Observe closely every detail, for a strategy reassessed over time.
  • Coordinate with the professionals around your child.
  • Move forward step by step, hand in hand with you.

For information and peer support between parents, the ASPEDAH association (French-speaking Swiss association of parents of children with attention deficit / hyperactivity) is a valuable resource.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an ADHD diagnosis to consult?

No. You can consult for attention difficulties, restlessness or impulsivity, even without a formal diagnosis. Depending on the situation, I may recommend that you see a doctor or specialist for an assessment.

Does homeopathy replace ADHD medication?

No. Any decision about medical treatment is made with the doctor who follows your child. Homeopathy is complementary and does not interfere with prescribed treatment.

Can parents come without the child?

Yes. The parent sessions can take place without the child, so that you can talk freely about daily life, your questions and your needs.

Do you also support teenagers and adults with ADHD?

Yes. ADHD can affect teenagers and adults, and the individualised approach of classical homeopathy adapts to every age.
