New Mothers & little ones

Baby: breastfeeding & sleep

The first months are wonderful… and sometimes terribly exhausting. I support mothers with breastfeeding and families with their baby’s sleep, gently, attentively and without judgement.

Book an appointment
Breastfeeding

Supporting mothers with breastfeeding

Breastfeeding is natural, but not always simple. You may recognise yourself if…

  • You have pain, cracked nipples or engorgement.
  • You are worried about your milk supply or your baby’s weight gain.
  • Feeds are difficult: your baby gets agitated, falls asleep at the breast or refuses it.
  • You feel exhausted, alone or discouraged.
  • You are preparing to go back to work or to wean gently.

How I can help

A space to take stock of your breastfeeding, practical guidance and individualised support — including homeopathic support — to help you live this period more calmly, alongside your midwife and paediatrician.

Baby’s sleep

Gentler nights for the whole family

Repeated waking, difficulty settling, evening crying… Lack of sleep weighs on the whole household.

  • Frequent night waking, broken nights.
  • Difficulty settling, a baby who only falls asleep in arms or at the breast.
  • Discomforts that disturb sleep: colic, reflux, teething.
  • Exhausted parents who no longer know what to try.

How I can help

Understanding your baby’s sleep according to their age and temperament, putting in place routines that suit your family, and relieving the discomforts that stop them sleeping well — with individualised follow-up that always respects your child’s rhythm.

What matters to me

A gentle, non-judgemental approach

Every mother, every baby, every family is unique. There is no ready-made recipe, only solutions adapted to you.

  • Listening to the whole story: pregnancy, birth, the first days.
  • Respecting your choices and your baby’s rhythm.
  • Offering practical guidance and close follow-up at the start.
  • Working in coordination with your midwife and paediatrician.
FAQ

Frequently asked questions

From what age can I consult for my baby?

From birth, including premature babies. Consultations are adapted to the little one’s age.

Is homeopathy compatible with breastfeeding?

Yes. Properly prescribed homeopathic remedies have no known contraindications, including during breastfeeding.

Does this support replace the midwife or paediatrician?

No. My support is complementary and always respects the medical care of both mother and baby. If in doubt (weight loss, fever, worrying signs), contact your paediatrician or midwife.

Important information. Homeopathy is a complementary medicine. It never replaces prescribed medical treatment or a consultation with a doctor or specialist. If in doubt or in an emergency, please contact your healthcare professional.
