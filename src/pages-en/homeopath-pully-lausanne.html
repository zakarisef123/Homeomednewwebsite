Practical information

Frequently asked questions about the Pully practice

Where is the entrance to the practice in Pully?

Place Neuve 1, in Pully’s pedestrian zone. The building entrance is at the back of the Visilab shop, on the playground side. The practice is on the 1st floor, accessible by lift or stairs.

Where can I park near the Pully practice?

The recommended car park is Pully-Centre (Migros Pully). Outdoor parking spaces are also available nearby.

How do I get there from Lausanne by public transport?

By train, the practice is a 1-minute walk from Pully station. By bus: Pully-Clergère stop (lines 9, 47, 49) or Pully-Centre (line 47), 3 minutes on foot; Gare stop (lines 8, 25, 47, 48), 5 minutes.

Which days do you see patients in Pully?

Mondays, Tuesdays and Wednesdays from 8am to 6pm, and Friday mornings from 8am to 1pm, by appointment only. On Thursdays I see patients in Fribourg.

Do you see children and babies in Pully?

Yes, from birth. There is a corner specially set up for little ones in the waiting room.

Do you consult in English?

Yes. I consult in English, French and Italian.
