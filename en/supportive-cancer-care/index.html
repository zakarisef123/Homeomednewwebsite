Supportive care & integrative oncology

Regain balance and stability during and after treatment

Specialised in supportive care in Pully/Lausanne and Fribourg, I support you with professionalism, attentiveness and kindness to help your body during and after your treatment protocols, alongside your medical care.

When body and life are turned upside down

A cancer diagnosis is a turning point

A before and an after. It comes as a real shock, for the person concerned and for their loved ones.

Then come the stages of the care pathway: surgery, treatments, medical appointments… Each phase puts the body, emotions and daily life to the test. Fatigue sets in, emotions get tangled, and loved ones look for ways to help, sometimes without knowing how.

Medical treatments are essential. Around them there is an equally fundamental need: to regain some balance, meaning and support — human, practical, at every step of the way.

That is exactly where I come in: to help you get through, understand and soften each stage of this journey, with kindness, method, attentiveness and coherence. My role is to work always alongside the medical team, never as a substitute.

How I can help

Personalised support, at your own pace

Every support plan is individualised, structured and evolves according to your needs, your reactions and your pace — always with complete respect for your treatment.

During treatment

Supporting body and mind to get through some stages of the care pathway more easily and better tolerate treatment side effects (chemotherapy, radiotherapy, hormone therapy, targeted therapies, immunotherapy):

  • Digestive problems: nausea, vomiting, diarrhoea, mouth ulcers, loss of appetite or taste
  • Peripheral neuropathy: tingling, pain in hands and feet
  • Hair loss, fragile skin (dryness, burning, redness, allergic reactions) or nails
  • Pain, muscle, bone or joint tension
  • Persistent fatigue, disturbed sleep
  • Emotional load: stress, anxiety, adjusting daily and social life

After treatment

The recovery and rehabilitation phase begins: regaining confidence, energy and movement. I help you to:

  • Support recovery: fatigue, sleep, vitality
  • Find a more peaceful relationship with your body: fragile self-image, physical discomfort, easing physical and psychological tension
  • Ease the return to daily life: gradually returning to work, activities and social life, at your own pace

Over the course of our work together, people often describe feeling better and more comfortable, feeling less alone, listened to and understood, and being able to find concrete bearings again.

My story

An approach born from a personal journey

Many of you ask me how I became a homeopath and why I chose to support people with cancer. Beyond my training and practice, my commitment to supportive care comes above all from a personal story. My father’s.

In 2017, when he had just been diagnosed with colon cancer, I discovered the notion of “supportive care” for the first time. I looked for ways to help him, to support him as best I could through the different stages of his treatment. That is how I came across homeopathy, and then chose to make it my profession…

This experience shaped my practice: demanding, rigorous, deeply human.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can homeopathy replace chemotherapy or radiotherapy?

No. Homeopathy is never an alternative to cancer treatment. It is used only as a complement, to support the patient on their journey.

Does homeopathy interact with or have contraindications to cancer treatments?

Used in a supportive-care setting, homeopathy does not alter the action of conventional treatments (chemotherapy, radiotherapy, hormone therapy, immunotherapy, targeted therapies). It can therefore be offered as a complement, without risk of drug interaction or contraindication, always as a complementary approach.

Can homeopathy be offered for chemotherapy-induced nausea?

It can be offered as support, within supportive care, to help you cope better with some of the discomforts linked to treatment, always in agreement with the medical team.

Can I consult during radiotherapy?

Homeopathic follow-up can be offered as support, to help manage discomforts such as fatigue, irritability, sleep problems or skin discomfort.

Can homeopathy help after cancer surgery?

It can be offered as support, always in coordination with the medical team, to help manage some post-operative discomforts and support recovery.

Can I be followed during hormone therapy (e.g. breast or prostate cancer)?

In this context, homeopathy can be offered as support for discomforts such as hot flushes, joint pain or fatigue, always alongside the cancer treatment.

Can I consult while on immunotherapy or targeted therapies?

Homeopathy does not alter the action of treatments, but it can help patients manage some common discomforts (fatigue, digestive or skin problems).

Does homeopathy also help on a mental and emotional level?

Homeopathic follow-up always takes emotional aspects into account (anxiety, sleep problems, loss of confidence). The aim is to provide global support, always alongside psychological or medical care.

Is it useful after treatment has ended?

Some patients choose to continue support after their treatment, to address overall recovery, stabilise their sleep, support their emotional balance and gradually regain confidence.

Important information. Homeopathy is a complementary medicine. It never replaces prescribed medical treatment or a consultation with a doctor or specialist. If in doubt or in an emergency, please contact your healthcare professional.

This support is part of supportive care and overall follow-up, consistent with and complementary to the medical care established by your healthcare team.
