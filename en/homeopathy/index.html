When symptoms keep coming back, again and again…

A child

who goes from one ear infection to the next, wakes at night or has behavioural difficulties…

A woman

0 to 18 years

Children & teens

I support children from the earliest age with common issues such as:

  • Recurrent ENT infections: colds, ear infections, bronchitis
  • Difficult nights and sleep, bedwetting / enuresis
  • Digestive and teething problems
  • ADHD, concentration difficulties, developmental or behavioural issues (impulsivity, hyperactivity…)
  • Skin problems: eczema, irritation, itching, allergies, acne

Rigorous homeopathy to bring lasting relief, strengthen the child’s overall balance, limit relapses and support development.

Women’s & hormonal health

I help women through the different stages of their hormonal life with a precise, structured approach:

  • Cycle problems, period pain and premenstrual syndrome
  • Pain: polycystic ovaries, endometriosis
  • Menopause and perimenopause
  • My baby & me: planning a pregnancy, difficulty conceiving, support during and after pregnancy, birth preparation, breastfeeding
  • Thyroid and hormonal problems, weight gain, loss of energy

Personalised work to regain rhythm, clarity and well-being in your body.

Children & adults

ENT, allergies & skin

I support adults and children with recurrent, chronic or allergy-prone conditions such as:

  • Skin rashes including eczema, psoriasis, hives and chronic dermatoses
  • ENT: rhinitis, sinusitis, ear infections
  • Asthma and breathing problems, allergic rhinitis
  • Sensitivities / seasonal allergies (hay fever), persistent fatigue linked to immune defences
  • Atopic terrain, unexplained skin reactions

Terrain-based homeopathy that looks for the pattern behind the repetition, to bring deep, lasting stability.

Alongside your medical team

Supportive cancer care

As a Swiss federally certified homeopath trained in supportive oncology, I offer structured, personalised and complementary support:

  • During treatment (surgery, chemo, radiotherapy, hormone therapy, immunotherapy), to better manage some of the discomforts you may experience
  • After treatment, during recovery, to support physical and emotional recuperation

Is it right for me?

Homeopathy is right for you or your child if…

  • You suffer from recurrent problems that persist despite treatment.
  • You are looking for a structured medicine, based on a precise analysis of your unique terrain.
  • You want holistic support that goes beyond symptoms and addresses the root cause.
  • You want to understand what your body (or your child’s) is expressing, and act with clarity.
  • You are looking for regular, progressive follow-up to achieve lasting results.

Move forward with method. Heal with meaning. Freed from your symptoms, you will gradually regain a freer, lighter life, closer to what you truly want.
