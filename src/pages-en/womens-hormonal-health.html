Menstrual cycles and period pain

The cycles that shape our lives

  • Your periods are heavy and painful, to the point of stopping you from working or studying.
  • Every cycle comes with migraines, intense fatigue or pelvic pain.
  • Premenstrual syndrome (PMS) disrupts your mood, appetite, sleep or energy.
  • Your cycle is irregular, unpredictable, very heavy or even haemorrhagic.

These symptoms shouldn’t be something you just have to put up with.

{{icon:heart}} How I can help

In consultation, I try to understand how your body experiences its cycle. Homeopathy can support a reduction in pain and cramps, a more regular cycle and the easing of PMS-related emotional symptoms.

PCOS and endometriosis

When PCOS or endometriosis complicate daily life

PCOS (polycystic ovary syndrome) and endometriosis can cause pelvic pain, very long or irregular cycles and difficulty conceiving.

  • Your cycles are very long, sometimes months apart.
  • You suffer from chronic pelvic pain.
  • You have been diagnosed with PCOS or endometriosis and are looking for complementary support.
  • Your wish for a child is made harder by these conditions.

{{icon:heart}} How I can help

Homeopathy does not replace essential medical care, but it can ease some pain, support the body in its search for balance and improve energy and mood day to day.

Fertility and trying to conceive

Wanting a child sometimes becomes a fertility journey

  • Your pregnancy is taking a long time and it is becoming a source of stress.
  • You are going through fertility treatment (IVF, stimulation) and looking for complementary support.
  • Your irregular cycles are reducing your chances of conceiving.
  • You feel discouraged, tired, sometimes alone on this journey.

{{icon:heart}} How I can help

The aim: to give the body more ease and readiness for conception. Supporting a more regular cycle, improving energy and vitality, complementing fertility treatment, and offering emotional support on what can be a difficult path.

Pregnancy, birth & after the birth

A pregnancy between joy and challenges

  • Nausea, reflux, fatigue, insomnia, pain during pregnancy.
  • Apprehension about the birth, anxiety.
  • Baby blues, painful breastfeeding, difficult recovery.

{{icon:heart}} How I can help

I offer follow-up that aims to relieve the discomforts of pregnancy, prepare body and mind for the birth, and support the postpartum period: preventing or easing the baby blues, supporting breastfeeding and helping recovery after the birth.

Perimenopause and menopause

A transition that deserves support

  • You are bothered by hot flushes and night sweats.
  • Your nights are disrupted by insomnia.
  • You feel constant fatigue and a drop in vitality.
  • Your mood is unstable; you feel more irritable or sensitive.
  • You experience vaginal dryness and a loss of intimate comfort.

{{icon:heart}} How I can help

Easing hot flushes, stabilising sleep and mood, regulating weight gain, relieving dryness and other intimate discomforts, regaining vitality and comfort day to day.

Thyroid and metabolism

When the thyroid upsets your energy

Hypothyroidism, hyperthyroidism, weight changes or unexplained fatigue: thyroid and hormonal disorders affect many women.

  • You suffer from hypothyroidism or hyperthyroidism.
  • Your weight changes for no clear reason.
  • You feel unexplained, persistent fatigue.
  • You are looking for complementary support to your medical care.

{{icon:heart}} How I can help

Alongside any necessary treatment, homeopathic follow-up can support the terrain, improve vitality and help you regain more stable energy and metabolism — always in addition to medical check-ups.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can homeopathy regulate an irregular cycle?

Yes. Homeopathy does not replace gynaecological care, but it can help regulate the cycle, ease pain and improve quality of life.

Can I consult if I want to have a baby?

Yes. Homeopathy can support a pregnancy plan, support fertility and prepare the body for conception, always in coordination with medical care.

Is homeopathy useful during pregnancy?

Yes. It can relieve discomforts (nausea, reflux, fatigue, insomnia), prepare for the birth and support the postpartum period (breastfeeding, baby blues, recovery).

Can I be followed during menopause?

Yes. It can soften hot flushes, improve sleep, stabilise mood and support energy.
