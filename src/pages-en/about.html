A few words about my approach

Working on the underlying terrain, not just the symptom

My commitment to classical homeopathy comes from a deep conviction: understand before treating. Understand why an imbalance sets in, and how to restore the coherence of the body as a whole.

Classical homeopathy allows me to support children, women and adults facing chronic, recurrent or complex conditions, by working on their underlying terrain — not just on the symptom.

Trained in Switzerland, abroad, and every year in India with some of the greatest practitioners, I have developed a personal method that is rigorous, alive and adapted to today’s realities.

Nothing set in stone: I respect tradition while adapting it to the modern world, so that every follow-up is concrete, evolving and deeply personalised.

For me, practising homeopathy is much more than a job: it is a way of bringing together science, clinical practice and humanity, with rigour and meaning.

My background

“I gave up (almost) everything for homeopathy!”

Swiss by adoption, I was born in Paris into a French-Italian family, and for a long time I worked internationally in law, finance and luxury.

But at 40, after a health scare, I started again from scratch to train in homeopathy — a discipline I had discovered several years earlier, when my father was diagnosed with colorectal cancer, and which allowed me to help him cope better with his treatment. Since then, it has become my passion and my purpose.

I chose the Swiss federal diploma, the most demanding and comprehensive training, so that I could rely on solid medical foundations and offer high-level care. I still continue my training today, particularly in India, with experienced practitioners — as one of my best friends says: “You’d go to the ends of the earth to find the best techniques!”

I have also completed diplomas in supportive cancer care and micronutrition, to better understand each patient’s terrain.

This work is much more than a career choice: it is a life choice, a choice with meaning, which I practise with commitment, humanity and rigour.
