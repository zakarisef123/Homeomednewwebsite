Frequently asked questions

Any questions?

Answers to the questions we hear most often before a first appointment.

All questions
Is it covered by my insurance?

Yes, by most Swiss supplementary health insurers. Paola Orlando is recognised by ASCA, RME, Visana and EGK.

Do I need to stop my medical treatment?

No. Homeopathy is complementary and does not interfere with your medication. We work alongside your doctor.

Is it suitable for babies and children?

Yes, from birth, including premature babies.

How long does a consultation last?

75 to 90 minutes for the first one, then 30 to 60 minutes for follow-ups.
