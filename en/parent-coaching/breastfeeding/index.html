Support for mothers

Breastfeeding support

Breastfeeding is natural, but not always easy. The first months are wonderful… and sometimes incredibly tough. I support you with your breastfeeding gently, attentively and without judgement.

Book an appointment
This may sound familiar if…

When breastfeeding becomes difficult

  • You have pain, cracked nipples or engorgement.
  • You worry about your milk supply or your baby’s weight gain.
  • Feeds are difficult: your baby fusses, falls asleep at the breast or refuses it.
  • You feel exhausted, alone or discouraged.
  • You are preparing to go back to work or to wean gently.
“Every feed hurts.”
“I’m afraid I don’t have enough milk.”
“I go back to work in a month – how will I manage?”
How I can help

Practical support, at your own pace

Take stock

A space to talk about your breastfeeding, your questions and your worries.

Practical guidance

Positions, feeding rhythm, day-to-day organisation: solutions suited to your situation.

Close follow-up

To help you live this period more calmly, alongside your midwife and paediatrician.

What matters to me

A gentle, non-judgemental approach

Every mother and every baby is unique. There is no one-size-fits-all recipe, only solutions that suit you.

  • Listening to the whole story: pregnancy, birth, first days.
  • Respecting your choices, including the choice to stop.
  • Offering practical landmarks and close follow-up at the start.
  • Working alongside your midwife and paediatrician.
FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When can I come to see you?

During pregnancy to prepare for breastfeeding, in the very first days, or later on (going back to work, weaning). It is never too early or too late to ask for help.

Does this replace the midwife or paediatrician?

No. My support is complementary and always respects the medical care of mother and baby. If in doubt (weight loss, fever, worrying signs), contact your paediatrician or midwife.

Can I bring my baby?

Of course. Your baby is welcome at the practice.

Can I be supported by video call?

Yes. Sessions can take place at the practice in Pully or Fribourg, or by video call anywhere in Switzerland.

Important information. This is an educational service for parents. It does not diagnose, is not psychotherapy and does not replace care from your paediatrician, midwife, a child psychiatrist or a psychologist. If in doubt or in an emergency, contact your healthcare professional.
