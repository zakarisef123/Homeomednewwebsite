Why I chose to support people with cancer: the story of my father, my hero

By Paola Orlando22 September 2025

This choice of specialty did not happen by chance. It was born from a personal story — that of my father, who went through cancer. He is the one who set me on this path.

Many of you ask me how I became a homeopath and why I chose to support people with cancer. I’m going to tell you my story — or rather his story, the story of a hero, my hero, my father, Cosimo Orlando. Because he is the one who led me down this path.

July 2017

Freshly back from a holiday in the United States with my husband, I called my parents in Paris as usual to tell them we had arrived safely in Switzerland. On the phone, my mother’s first words left no room for doubt:

“Paola, are you sitting down?… Your father has cancer.”

He had just been diagnosed with colon cancer. And then: emptiness, a black hole.

In an instant your life is turned upside down, takes on another rhythm, as if suspended. You enter a parallel dimension, that of illness and its treatments, tumour markers, PET scans. You realise that it doesn’t only happen to other people… Suddenly everything follows in quick succession: the diagnosis, emergency surgery, and the start of treatment — for Dad, chemotherapy.

At the time I wasn’t yet a homeopath; I didn’t even think about it. My fields were law, luxury, finance, private banking, economics, politics. My playground was international. But for some years I had been treating myself with homeopathy, self-taught, because I had always been drawn to natural medicine. Not to the point of making it my job, though!

Looking for ways to help him

With the start of chemo came the first side effects (no judgement, only lived experience), and each of us — my mother, my sister, my brother and I — looked for a way to help Dad, to support him, to relieve him. So naturally I started looking into homeopathy, and I discovered that it was part of supportive care in oncology, a term I was discovering at the same time.

So I learned… alone… I started, self-taught. Then I had the immense good fortune to discover the excellent work of Dr Lionel Bagot, a homeopathic doctor in Strasbourg, a great specialist and pioneer of supportive care in oncology, and founder of SHISSO. He supported me in my learning to help Dad, advised me, taught me, guided me.

All those years, I worked closely with my father’s oncologist, a wonderful doctor, who often said to me:

“Paola, I don’t know what you’re giving your father, I don’t understand any of it, but it really helps him. And that’s what matters.”

With each change of treatment (chemo, then targeted therapies, then chemo again) or each new operation, I adapted, still self-taught. But we kept moving forward. Dad was able to get through his treatments without interrupting his care pathway, and that was what mattered.

2019: the RESET button

Then in 2019 I had a health scare… I had just turned 40, at the height of my career… so I thought it over and said to myself: “Pao, you need to change something, or this is going to end badly!”

So I pressed the RESET button and gave up everything, or almost — I kept my family, the dog, my friends and of course my dear husband — for homeopathy. I left everything to start again from scratch. My choice was clear: to become a homeopath and work in supportive cancer care.

Alongside my Swiss federal diploma in homeopathy, I trained in supportive oncology. In 2022 I obtained my University Diploma in Clinical Sciences in Cancer Care at Gustave Roussy, the leading cancer research centre in Europe and in the world’s top 5. I did my final internship at Gustave Roussy in Villejuif in digestive oncology (of course), a department led by the eminent Prof. Michel Ducreux, and in palliative care.

Then, still to help Dad, I continued my homeopathy training. I turned to India, which has had some of the greatest homeopaths in oncology for generations. I learned the method of Dr A.U. Ramakrishnan, then asked for help from and learned with Dr P. Banerji in Kolkata, and above all with my dear friend, the homeopathic doctor Dr Gaurang Gaikwad, who helped me a great deal with Dad and also welcomed me to his clinic in Mumbai. Because as my best friend says: “You’d go to the ends of the earth to train” — and that is what I did, and still do.

And today, I carry on. And tomorrow, I will carry on, always.

So now you know everything

I owe homeopathy and supportive care to my hero. Without these twists of fate, I wouldn’t be where I am today.

This is my story, his story, OUR story. Raw, personal, without frills or detours. It isn’t the homeopath’s e-pen writing, but that of the novice daughter of 2017 who looked for ways to help her father. I found them in homeopathy, which I love more than anything, and I decided to make it my profession.

I didn’t choose homeopathy and supportive cancer care by chance, but out of conviction and life experience.

To you, my hero, thank you! Without you I wouldn’t be who I am today.
Forever,
Your daughter, Paola
