Frequently asked questions

Have questions? That’s normal!

Here are the answers to the most common questions. For anything else, don’t hesitate to get in touch.

Classical homeopathy

What does classical homeopathy treat?

Classical homeopathy can support many chronic, recurrent or acute conditions, in children as well as adults. It works in depth, taking into account how you function as a whole (terrain, reactivity, history, emotions…). It does not replace medical treatment, but can complement it within a structured, rigorous and personalised approach.

Which conditions are most often seen at the practice?
  • Recurrent problems in children: ENT, sleep, digestion, skin, behaviour…
  • Women’s and hormonal imbalances: cycle problems, menopause, endometriosis, pain, wanting a baby and support before, during and after pregnancy, thyroid disorders…
  • Skin, ENT, respiratory and allergic conditions: eczema, psoriasis, hives, herpes, molluscum, asthma, rhinitis, sinusitis, allergies…
  • Supportive cancer care: global support before, during and after conventional treatment.
  • Acute homeopathic care: winter infections, fever, ear infections, sore throats, cystitis, stomach bugs, coughs…
  • Chronic metabolic imbalances: persistent fatigue, chronic stress, sleep problems, autoimmune and chronic inflammatory diseases, digestive problems…
Is homeopathy suitable for children, teenagers, newborns and babies?

Yes. Classical homeopathy is very well suited to newborns, children and teenagers. Consultations are of course adapted to their age: observation, listening to the parents, play… I regularly see children for ENT, digestive, sleep, skin or behavioural problems.

Does it also work for emotional difficulties?

Yes. Homeopathy takes into account your physical and emotional symptoms. Stress, anxiety, nervous fatigue, hypersensitivity, irritability, sleep problems… can all be addressed seriously and methodically.

Are there any contraindications or side effects?

Properly prescribed homeopathic remedies have no known side effects and no contraindications, including in children, the elderly or people on heavy treatment. Homeopathy works gently but in a targeted way, without toxicity or dependence.

Can I come even without a precise medical diagnosis?

Yes. You can consult for a feeling, a discomfort or a recurring imbalance, even without a formal diagnosis. Depending on your situation, I may nevertheless recommend that you see your GP first if I think it necessary. Homeopathy analyses how you function as a whole, not just the symptoms.

Can homeopathy help me if I’ve already tried many other things?

Yes! Homeopathy is not based on a single protocol, but on a precise reading of how you, specifically, function. That is what allows complex or chronic situations to be approached differently.

Homeopathy & medicine

Do I need to stop my medical treatment?

No! Homeopathy does not replace medical treatment. It can, however, help you understand your situation better, stabilise your terrain and act in depth. Any decision to stop or adjust medication must be made with your GP.

Does homeopathy replace prescribed medical treatment or seeing a doctor?

No! Homeopathy is a complementary medicine. It can never replace or be an alternative to medical treatment already prescribed or to a consultation with a doctor or specialist. If in doubt, please contact your GP.

Can I take homeopathy at the same time as medical treatment?

Yes, absolutely! Classical homeopathy can be taken alongside any conventional treatment. It does not interfere with the action of medicines or disrupt their effect. If needed, we can adjust your follow-up in coordination with your GP.

Do you work with doctors or other health professionals?

Yes, absolutely! I work with doctors and health professionals when the situation requires it. I am convinced that well-designed complementarity is a real asset for therapeutic success.

Can I consult just for an acute problem?

Yes. Do you have acute symptoms that need prompt support? Book an acute homeopathy consultation. Reminder: in case of doubt or a medical emergency, please contact your family doctor.

The first consultation lasts 75 to 90 minutes; adult follow-ups last 45 to 60 minutes. It is an essential time to understand in depth how you function, analyse your symptoms, history and terrain, and lay the foundations for personalised, coherent follow-up.

Are consultations reimbursed?

I am recognised by ASCA, RME, Visana and EGK. Consultations are reimbursed by most Swiss supplementary health insurers. Simply check with your insurer, giving them my name: Paola Orlando, homeopath.

How often should I come, and for how long?

It depends on your situation, but generally every 4 to 6 weeks at the start, then further apart depending on your progress. The aim is to follow your progress rigorously and adjust the treatment at each stage. Follow-up ends when the expected results are achieved. A pause may also be suggested if it helps to observe how things evolve.

How long will it take to feel better?

It all depends on you, your initial reason for consulting, your history and what you are going through. But if we work together, we won’t be standing still. No promises or magic treatments, but a clear, precise path, adjusted at each step. With method. With humanity. And results that last.
