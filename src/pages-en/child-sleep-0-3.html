Parent coaching · 0 – 3 years

Child sleep 0–3 years: gentler nights

Repeated night waking, trouble falling asleep, evening crying… Lack of sleep weighs on the whole household. I help you understand your little one’s sleep and find guidance that suits your family, gently and without judgement.

{{rdv:Prendre rendez-vous}}
This may sound familiar if…

Nights that exhaust the whole family

  • Frequent night waking, broken nights.
  • Difficulty falling asleep, a child who only falls asleep in your arms or at the breast.
  • Short or difficult naps, a daily rhythm that’s hard to find.
  • Bedtimes that drag on, evening crying.
  • Exhausted parents who don’t know what else to try.
“He’s been waking every two hours for months.”
“Bedtime takes more than an hour every evening.”

How I can help

Guidance suited to your child and your family

Understand

Look at your child’s sleep in the light of their age and temperament, and at your days and nights.

Put in place

Routines and practical landmarks that suit your family and your values.

Adjust

Individual follow-up, always respecting your child’s rhythm, to adjust step by step.

What matters to me

A gentle, non-judgemental approach

Every child and every family is unique. There is no one-size-fits-all recipe, only solutions that suit you.

  • Listening to the whole story: birth, first months, current rhythm.
  • Respecting your choices and your child’s rhythm.
  • Offering practical landmarks and close follow-up at the start.
  • Working alongside your paediatrician.
FAQ

Frequently asked questions

From what age can I come to see you?

From birth up to 3 years, including for premature babies. The support is adapted to your child’s age.

Is the support with or without the child?

It is aimed at parents. Depending on age and situation, your little one can come along to the session.

Does this replace the paediatrician?

No. It is complementary and always respects your child’s medical care. If in doubt (fever, weight loss, worrying signs), contact your paediatrician.

Can I be supported by video call?

Yes. Sessions can take place at the practice in Pully or Fribourg, or by video call anywhere in Switzerland.
