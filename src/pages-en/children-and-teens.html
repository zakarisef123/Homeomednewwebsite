Newborns (including premature) & babies

The first months

Wonderful… but sometimes terribly exhausting. I often see worn-out parents who describe:

  • Colic, reflux, spitting up, digestive problems: every feed becomes an ordeal.
  • Difficult sleep: sleepless nights, endless crying, the whole house exhausted.
  • Repeated infections: ear infections, bronchiolitis, frequent worrying fevers.
  • Reactive skin: rashes, eczema, baby acne, redness.
  • Slow development: difficult weight gain, growth to monitor.
  • Painful teething: burning cheeks, screaming, fever — how can you soothe your baby?

{{icon:heart}} How I can help

In the first consultation, I take the time to listen to the whole story: conception, pregnancy, birth, the first months. Together, we try to understand how your baby functions as a whole.

I propose an individualised homeopathic treatment, always adapted to your baby’s age and vitality, often with close follow-up at the start so we can adjust gently as we go.

Children

As your child grows

New challenges appear:

  • Recurrent ENT infections: ear infections, colds, coughs that keep coming back.
  • Sensitive skin: eczema, itching, seasonal allergies that disrupt daily life.
  • Restless sleep: nightmares, night waking, difficulty falling asleep.
  • ADHD, poor concentration, hyperactivity, impulsivity: comments from school, tension at home.
  • Unsettled digestion: tummy aches, constipation, diarrhoea.
  • Persistent fatigue: low energy, struggling to keep up at school.

When these symptoms keep recurring, they end up affecting not only the child, but the whole family.

{{icon:heart}} How I can help

With classical homeopathy, I don’t just “treat a symptom”. I try to understand how your child functions: their rhythm, their reactions, their temperament. This allows me to propose a personalised treatment that works in depth to stabilise their terrain.

The aim: for them to fall ill less often, recover faster, regain stable energy and a smoother daily life. And for you, as parents: less worry, fewer broken nights, and the joy of watching your child grow up more serenely.

Teenagers

A pivotal time

New imbalances appear and weigh on the body as much as on self-confidence. I am regularly consulted for:

  • Acne and lack of confidence: a source of self-consciousness and withdrawal.
  • Allergies and rhinitis: persistent symptoms that disrupt school life.
  • Hormonal changes: painful periods and irregular cycles in girls, physical and emotional upheavals in girls and boys.
  • Sleep problems and fatigue: insomnia, chronic fatigue, difficulty concentrating, screen addiction.
  • Emotions on edge: hypersensitivity to stress, self-image, how others see them.

{{icon:heart}} How I can help

My role is to offer the teenager a space where they feel listened to and understood. With suitable follow-up, homeopathy supports both the body and emotional balance. Little by little, symptoms ease, vitality returns and confidence is rebuilt.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to see an improvement with homeopathy in a child?

Every child is different. Sometimes the effects are felt very quickly; at other times it takes several weeks or months to consolidate the results.

Does homeopathy replace the doctor / paediatrician?

No. My role is complementary: I always work with respect for your child’s medical care.

Can I also see you for a fever, cough, ear infection or stomach bug?

Yes. Homeopathy can be useful in some acute situations (fever, ear infection, cough…), but medical advice remains essential in an emergency.

Can homeopathy help a child with recurrent ear infections?

Yes, many parents consult me for recurrent ear infections, sometimes from the moment their child starts nursery or school. Homeopathy supports the child as a whole, taking into account their terrain and the way they react to infections. The aim is not only to relieve pain, but also to reduce the frequency and intensity of episodes, alongside paediatric care.

Can I consult for a baby’s or teenager’s eczema?

Yes, eczema is a common reason for consultation. In babies it can appear in the first months and disrupt sleep or feeding. In adolescence it often has a significant impact on self-confidence. I seek to understand your child’s overall terrain in order to propose an individualised treatment that aims to calm flare-ups and improve quality of life, alongside dermatological care if needed.
